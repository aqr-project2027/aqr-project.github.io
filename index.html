Generate, query, refine

AQR first generates an action sequence with a diffusion policy, then queries local geometry along the candidate tool trajectory. A confidence-weighted, bounded residual refines the completed action sequence once.

This source showcase presents the model and its training objective. Experiment configurations, calibration data, datasets and checkpoints are not included.

Trajectory examples

Selected successful trajectories assembled from historical simulation render snapshots, played at 5 fps. No intermediate actions or frames were generated.
